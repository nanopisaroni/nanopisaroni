Personal Pantheon

A mental map of 16 thinkers who shaped me

This book is not a summary of philosophy or a list of Western thought's greatest hits. It's something else: a personal pantheon. A collection of 16 people who think (or thought) in a way that made me think differently.

Each chapter is a living profile: who they are, what ideas define them, what to read to get started, how they connect to others in the pantheon, and — most importantly — concepts you can take into action. It's not meant to be memorized. It's meant to be kept close, revisited, discussed.

It starts with Borges, ends with Facundo Cabral. In between: Feynman, Taleb, Deutsch, Thiel, Popper, Munger, Kahneman, Paul Graham, Naval Ravikant, Yudkowsky, the Stoics, Alan Watts, Krishnamurti. Each one is there for a precise reason: they gave me a mental tool I still use.

268 pages. Written from Córdoba, Argentina, in 2026. With obsession, patience, and the certainty that the pantheon is never finished.
